feat(details): revision toast + resolve /details from preferred USGS source" \
  -m "Problem
-------
Old /details/:id links were already resolvable after PHIVOLCS revises an
event (committed earlier), but users got no indication the data they were
looking at had changed, and the whole pipeline silently ignored the USGS
source preference:

1. When an old link resolved to a revised event, the page silently showed
   the latest data with no notice that the magnitude/origin/epicenter had
   been updated.
2. In USGS mode, /details/:id still resolved exclusively against PHIVOLCS:
   the live lookup called fetchPhivolcsData (never USGS) and archive
   lookups used the PHIVOLCS archives, so a USGS event link could not be
   found and fell through to 'Earthquake Details Unavailable'. USGS events
   could therefore never show the revision notice.
3. USGS events have negative longitudes (e.g. 36.57--121.18). The base64
   slug decoder split on '-', producing an empty segment that dropped the
   latitude and stripped the longitude sign, corrupting both the revision
   comparison and the fuzzy-match coordinate gate for any USGS link.
